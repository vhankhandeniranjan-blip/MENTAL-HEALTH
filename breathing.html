Take a gentle breathing break

Follow the circle if it feels comfortable. Breathe gently and naturally—there is no need to take unusually deep breaths or match the timing perfectly.

ReadyPress start

The guide uses a comfortable 4-second inhale and 4-second exhale, with no breath-holding. You can stop at any time and breathe normally.

If you feel dizzy, uncomfortable or short of breath, stop the exercise and return to your normal breathing.
