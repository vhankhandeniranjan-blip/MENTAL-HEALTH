UNDERSTANDING THE JOURNEY

Challenges are part of life.
You don't have to face them alone.

Teenage years can bring changes in school, friendships, family life and emotions. Naming a challenge can be a first step toward finding support.

📚

Academic pressure

Exams, expectations and busy schedules can feel demanding. Break tasks into smaller steps and speak with a teacher or trusted adult if pressure feels too high.

🫂

Friendship changes

Friendships can change over time. Healthy relationships include respect, listening, boundaries and room to be yourself.

🌦️

Strong emotions

Feelings can be complicated. Try naming what you feel and sharing it with someone you trust rather than judging yourself.

📱

Social media pressure

Online comparisons do not show the whole story. Take breaks from content that leaves you feeling pressured or upset.

🏡

Family expectations

It can be difficult when expectations feel different. Choose a calm moment to explain your point of view and listen to theirs.

🧭

Uncertainty about the future

You do not need every answer right now. Explore interests gradually and ask supportive adults for guidance.

When a challenge feels too big

Talk to a parent or guardian, teacher, school counsellor or another trusted adult. If you feel unsafe or there is immediate danger, seek adult help right away.
