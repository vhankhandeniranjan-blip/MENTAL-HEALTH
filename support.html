YOU DESERVE SUPPORT

Talking can be a strong first step.

You do not need to wait until a problem feels huge before asking for help. Choose someone safe, trusted and willing to listen.

Parent, guardian or family member

Choose an adult you feel comfortable speaking with. You can start with: “Something has been on my mind. Can we talk?”

Teacher or school counsellor

A teacher, counsellor or another trusted school adult may help you think through next steps and connect you with support.

Qualified health professional

If worries persist or affect everyday life, a qualified health professional can help assess what support may be useful.

Immediate safety concerns in India

If someone is in immediate danger, tell a trusted adult now and call 112 with adult support. Tele-MANAS (14416) provides mental-health support in India.

A reminder about privacy

When using online tools, avoid sharing your full name, address, school, phone number or other identifying details. An AI chatbot is not a counsellor and cannot provide emergency care.
