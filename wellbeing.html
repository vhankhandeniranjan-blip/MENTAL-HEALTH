SMALL STEPS, GENTLE ROUTINES

Care for your mind.
At your own pace.

Wellbeing is not about being happy all the time. It is about noticing what you need and building routines that support you.

🌙

Rest regularly

Try to keep a steady sleep routine and give yourself breaks during busy days.

🚶

Move in ways you enjoy

A walk, a game or gentle stretching can be a refreshing change of pace.

🥗

Eat and hydrate regularly

Regular meals and water help support your everyday energy and concentration.

📝

Name your feelings

Writing a few words about your day can help you understand what is on your mind.

🤝

Stay connected

Spend time with people who treat you with kindness and respect.

🎨

Make room for fun

Creative hobbies, music, reading and play can give your day a positive pause.

A gentle breathing pause

Try breathing comfortably and naturally. There is no need to force or hold your breath. Stop if you feel uncomfortable.
